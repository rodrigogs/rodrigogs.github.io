# Surface brief: home (rodrigogs.github.io)

Scope: the one-page personal site at `/` (EN) and `/pt/` (PT-BR), plus the GitHub profile README as a sibling surface in the same world. Visitor mode: **Persuade**. Build path: code-led (no image generation on this machine).

Audience and job: hiring managers, tech leads and technical recruiters for senior/staff roles; decide in under a minute, then verify depth. Action: email (copy or mailto), LinkedIn, GitHub. Proof: fetched GitHub/npm/crates.io numbers, live products, upstream contributions, public LinkedIn timeline. Constraints: PRODUCT.md.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is Rodrigo's release history. Every claim is a dated entry with a version and a link to its proof. It refuses the dev-portfolio default (hero, bento cards, skill-badge wall, dark neon) and its editorial-serif opposite.

OWN-WORLD: Keep a Changelog and release-page grammar on a bright white sheet with near-black ink. Four named release roles form the whole palette: Added green, Changed amber, Fixed cobalt and Deprecated vermilion. They appear only as solid tag plates, diffstat blocks and the compare view. Archivo carries the type across its widths: expanded black for release titles and CalVer numerals, normal width for notes. Martian Mono is reserved for versions, dates, hashes and counts. Hairline rules separate entries, and a left time rail carries tag plates.

STORY: The visitor learns that he is a senior engineer who ships whole products and works on agent infrastructure now. The linked numbers make that believable. Then they copy the email or open LinkedIn.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left seven columns: the green tag plate "2026.09.28 · Latest", then the name in expanded black (about 5.5rem), a one-sentence release title and three tagged notes with proof numbers. The primary "Email me" plate sits below them, with GitHub beside it, and LinkedIn too once the owner confirms its URL. Right five columns: the career version rail from 2010 to now, with the current role lit and a live compare readout.

FORM: Release notes / changelog, #4 of 7 on my list, seed eee612c4. The signature interaction is Compare: pick any two years and the page renders a +/− diff of stack and work computed from real repo data. The compare state lives in the URL.

RAISES:
- From the industrial quote grammar: literal labeling. Every region is named by its plain noun (Now, Work, Upstream, Packages, Career, Toolchain, Contact), so wayfinding is reading.
- From the azulejo station hall: a whole-unit grid. Every block snaps to whole rows of a 4px baseline on 12 columns; nothing is fractional.
- From the Fletcher poster: one idea per section. Each section opens with a single claim sentence that stands without its body.
- From the character goods catalog: fixed furniture. Every release entry carries identical fields (name, born, latest version, note, proof number, links), and dormant projects print in a retired state.
- From the HyperCard stack: everything is addressable. Every entry has a permalink anchor, like a release tag.
- From the collider event display: marks encode quantities. Every bar, block and weight maps to a measured number in tabular figures; there are no decorative marks.

Challenger verdicts (unattended run, the user asked for no questions): all six were declined on audience identification and product clarity against the changelog world. Each one's kept discipline is the raise above.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
